What the test taught us

Every change below comes from a number in the Creative Test analysis: Aug 31–Oct 15, $2,162.77 spent, 19 form leads.

  1. Videos 1 and 2 (files named “AI 1”) produced all 7 practice leads and all 4 booked calls; Videos 3 and 4 (“AI 2”) produced 9 leads and no practices

    Retire Videos 3 and 4; build the video test on Video 2

  2. Video 2 went 3 for 3: every lead was a practice that booked a call, and it held viewers longest (4.6 s average watch vs 2.3 s for Video 4)

    Video 2 is the control; four new hooks are cut onto its body

  3. Average watch time was 4 seconds and only 10% of viewers reached the 25% mark

    Every new hook names orthodontists and dentists by second 2–5, in voice-over or on-screen text, so the wrong viewers drop off early

  4. The test only ran video, and only the ad-account message; the full-service story (ads, call center, follow-up, booked consults) was never tested

    A second ad set tests five full-service static concepts

  5. All four ads shared one ad set’s $50/day budget, and Meta moved that spend from Video 2 (45% → 29%) to Video 4 (8% → 41%)

    Video and statics each get their own $40/day ad set, so one format can’t starve the other

  6. The open lead form let non-practices through (8 of 17)

    Higher Intent form with a role question, practice name and website

  7. 4 of 7 practices booked a call within minutes of submitting

    Keep the existing meeting-booking CTA on the completion screen; it’s working

  8. Reels drove 14 of 19 leads; Stories, Reels overlay and in-stream spent $367.32 for 1

    Run on Facebook and Instagram Reels and Feeds only

  9. All 7 practices came from five states; every booked call came from California and Pennsylvania

    Stay national for the test; add a California and Pennsylvania ad set once the winners are known

  10. Only 6,399 people were reached, at a frequency of 2.0

    Add retargeting once the new video-viewer audiences have built up

Instant form

Decision rules, reviewed every Monday against the GHL lead list

  • Pause an ad

    Once it has spent $300, twice the target cost per practice, without a practice lead.

  • Drop a hook

    If, after 3,000 impressions, fewer viewers reach 25% than on the Video 2 control.

  • Drop a static

    If, after 3,000 impressions, its click-through rate is under half the ad set's average.

  • Compare the two ad sets

    On cost per practice and per booked call each Monday, and move up to $20/day toward the one with the lower cost per practice (neither drops below $20/day).

  • Scale

    Raise an ad set's budget 20% every 4 days while its 7-day cost per practice is $150 or less; hold between $150 and $250; cut 20% above $250.

  • Refresh creative

    Every 4–6 weeks, or sooner if frequency passes 3.
